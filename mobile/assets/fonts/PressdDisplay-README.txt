PressdDisplay-*.ttf are built from Allo (Josh Puckett, SIL OFL 1.1 — Allo-OFL.txt), instanced
sharp (ROND 0) at optical size 28 and weights 700 / 800, with the digits and number punctuation
of Bricolage Grotesque 800 (Mathieu Triay, SIL OFL 1.1, Google Fonts) merged in, so one face
draws titles in Allo and scores in Bricolage. Named "Pressd Display", not Allo, as the OFL asks
of a modified version. Onest-*.ttf are Onest (Google Fonts, SIL OFL 1.1), static weights.
